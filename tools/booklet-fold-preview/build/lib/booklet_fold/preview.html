1. Turn through the folded booklet

These pages are cropped from the exported sheet images. Follow the links beneath them to locate the matching sheet side. Numbers mean source PDF positions, not printed page labels.

Fold each sheet along the green guide; nest sheet 1 outside sheet 2 and so on. Stack completed signatures in numerical order. Covers are shown as single pages.

2. Inspect the flat sheets

Both sides are viewed face-on and upright. Flip around the short edge of the landscape paper. Click a page number to find it in the folded view. Green guides appear only in this preview.

3. Print a small trial first

  1. Open booklet.pdf, choose the matching paper size and landscape orientation.
  2. Print at actual size / 100%, one PDF page per side. Disable printer booklet, n-up, auto-rotation, and fit-to-page options.
  3. Use duplex flip on short edge. Print only PDF pages 1–2 for a one-sheet trial; fold it and check orientation before printing the rest.
  4. Keep front/back pairs together. Fold and nest sheets separately within each signature; assemble signatures in order.

Digital validation does not establish your printer's feed behavior, registration, or printable area. Physical printing has not been performed. Manual duplex reinsertion depends on your printer; follow its manual and trial one sheet.
