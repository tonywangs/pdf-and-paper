Supported documents & privacy

Static PDFs only. Forms, non-link annotations and custom UserUnit pages are rejected. Links are omitted. Page boxes and existing rotations are retained. Outlines, attachments, tags and document metadata are not copied. Max 10 MiB/file, 40 MiB total, 200 pages/file and 300 output pages. Saved projects contain the plan and hashes, not PDF bytes; re-import matching originals on another browser. Recovery stores originals locally in IndexedDB; Clear desk removes them. This is not a sanitizer for hostile PDFs.
