How to review · limits & privacy

Page numbers are 1-based PDF positions, including cover sheets. Suggested pairs are never a claim of identity. Review ambiguous pages and correct the later-page selection; assigning an occupied page releases its previous pair. Unmatched pages may indicate insertion, deletion, or content reflow.

72 dpi raster rendering with PDF.js. Pink marks pixels whose largest RGB-channel change exceeds the tolerance (0–255). No alignment or noise removal. White padding preserves page size differences. Scans have visual matching only, no OCR. Similar appearance is not semantic or byte identity.

Up to 100 pages and 16 MiB per PDF, 2 million pixels per page, 120 million across both documents. Encrypted files are refused, including empty-password encryption. Malformed files may fail or be recovered by PDF.js; inspect its output. Visible annotations and saved form appearances are rendered; interactive forms, scripts, attachments, layers and hidden metadata are not reviewed. PDFs and review data stay in this browser's IndexedDB; clearing browser data removes recovery. Export contains rendered content, filenames and hashes. Use Forget comparison on shared machines.
